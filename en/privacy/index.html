Online friend room privacy

Jankobo is browser mahjong with no account and no login. A solo match runs entirely inside your browser, and the one place that needs a server is the online friend room. This page says what reaches that server and what it does not keep.

Play Jankobo

What reaches the server

When you enter an online friend room, your nickname and your connecting IP reach the server. The nickname is collected to show who you are to the three other players at the table: it appears on their screens as typed, and when the match ends it is kept as a name in those three players' saved replays. The connecting IP is used only as far as the browser and the server need it to talk to each other while the connection is open, and it is neither used for anything else nor kept.

A summary of your online record (matches played and how many times you placed 1st to 4th) is also passed through the server to the people in the same room. During a match, tapping an opponent's portrait shows theirs. The numbers are the record kept in your own browser; the server keeps them in the room record and deletes them along with it.

Pictures shown in a friend room

If you pick your profile portrait or one of your own characters as your face in a friend room, that portrait is uploaded to the server and shown on the screens of the people in the same room. If the host turns on Shared look when creating the room, the host's theme colors, character names and pictures (tiles, table and character portraits) are also uploaded to the server and shown to the people in the same room.

An uploaded portrait is deleted as soon as you leave your seat or change your face in the waiting room; the other pictures and the theme are deleted together with the room record. The room record is deleted as soon as everyone has left, or 24 hours after the match ends for a finished room. Any picture that could not be deleted then is removed by the server's storage rule once 3 days have passed since it was uploaded.

What the server log does not keep

The server log keeps no room code, no nickname and no connecting IP. More precisely, this server writes no application log of its own at all.

Where the replays live

A replay, the record of a finished match, is stored only inside your own browser. The room record the server was holding is deleted 24 hours after the match ends.

No account, no login, no tracking

There is no account to register and no login. There is no tracking that follows you around and recognises you.

A room code is an invite link, not a secret. Anyone who knows the code can walk into that room, so share it only with the people you mean to play with.

Visit statistics

How often each screen is opened is counted by GoatCounter. It stays the same cookieless setup as before, and room codes and nicknames never go there either.
