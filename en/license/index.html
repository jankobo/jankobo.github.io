Resources made with AI

Some of the illustrations, voices and background music were made with generative AI services. For example, OpenAI's image generation model (gpt-image) was used for pictures and ElevenLabs for the character voices.

The CC BY-NC 4.0 grant above covers the rights jankobo holds. When you reuse these resources, the terms of service of the service that made them may apply as well.

Your own themes and profile

Pictures, names and lines you add to a custom theme or your profile are not Jankobo's art or sound, so these licenses do not cover them. The rights belong to whoever made them (you, if you made them yourself), and you are responsible for registering and using them. In the app, the Your responsibility note next to the theme save button and in the My Profile window explains this in detail.

Your themes and profile are saved on this device. A portrait you pick as your face in a friend room, and the host's theme in a room with Shared look turned on, are uploaded to the server so the people in that room can see them.
